Keep it funny, keep it safe

Submission rules

Submissions may be edited, rejected, archived or deleted if they create privacy, professionalism, legal or workplace risk.

Allowed

  • Harmless jokes about shift life, pens, socks, coffee, badge reels, placement nerves and laundry piles.
  • General tips for students, new grads and healthcare workers.
  • Scrub swap details that do not expose private information.

Not allowed

  • Patient stories, even if you think they are de-identified.
  • Patient images, clinical details, diagnoses, treatment details or incident details.
  • Hospital, ward, clinic, employer, colleague or patient identifiers.
  • Bullying, harassment, discrimination, sexual content, hate speech or workplace call-outs.
  • Clinical advice, treatment claims or testimonials about regulated health services.
