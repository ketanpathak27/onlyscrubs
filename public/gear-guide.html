Gear Guide

What to look for before spending money on shift gear.

Scrubs

  • Check workplace or placement colour and uniform rules first.
  • Choose pockets, fabric and fit for safe movement.
  • Prioritise washability, comfort and opacity under bright lights.

Shoes

  • Follow local requirements for closed toes, wipeable materials and slip resistance.
  • Try shoes late in the day when feet are less optimistic.
  • Break new shoes in before a long shift.

Small gear

  • Black pens that actually write.
  • A cleanable water bottle.
  • A plain badge reel if allowed.
