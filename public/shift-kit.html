Shift tools

Shift Kit

Checklists, reset prompts and general structures for workdays that try to become personality tests.

Handover prompt

  • What changed?
  • What needs follow-up?
  • What is time-sensitive?
  • What have you already escalated?
  • What should not be forgotten when everyone gets busy?

Night shift reset

  • Hydrate before caffeine becomes your entire care plan.
  • Pack real food if possible.
  • Plan the drive home before you are cooked.
  • Use workplace fatigue processes if you are unsafe to drive.
