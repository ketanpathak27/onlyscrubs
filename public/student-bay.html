Placement prep

Student Bay

For students, new grads and anyone trying to look calm while internally buffering.

Placement packing checklist

  • Approved uniform or scrubs.
  • Closed shoes that meet local requirements.
  • Student ID, placement documents and required checks.
  • Lunch, water bottle, pens and a small notebook if allowed.
  • A plan for transport, parking and who to contact if delayed.

First-shift etiquette

  • Arrive early enough to find the right place.
  • Ask before documenting or using local systems.
  • Do not post workplace content online.
  • Escalate uncertainty early.
