Graduate programs are jobs with extra support. They are not one national form, and they are not a condition of registration. This hub is general information only. It does not rank employers, and it does not reproduce anyone's interview questions. Those questions change, and copying them would be a stale script rather than a preparation.

The longer state-by-state notes, including the smaller jurisdictions, are in [graduate programs by state](/guides/graduate-programs-by-state.html). Registration is separate: [AHPRA registration as a new grad](/guides/ahpra-registration-new-grad.html).

## How to use the table

The table is built from a single list of notes, each with the date we checked it. If a handbook and this page disagree, the handbook is right. Opening and closing dates are the thing that moves every year. We have not pasted a closing time that we could not read on the official page.

## New South Wales

NSW Health runs GradStart. The [recruitment handbook](https://www.health.nsw.gov.au/nursing/employment/Pages/gradstart-recruitment-handbook.aspx) is the document for the current round. The [eligibility page](https://www.health.nsw.gov.au/nursing/employment/gradstart/Pages/eligibility.aspx) for a 2027 start asks whether you complete your degree between 1 June 2026 and 31 May 2027, whether you can be registered before you start, and whether you can work. That window is about eligibility, not the day the form opens. Get the opening and closing dates from the handbook for the round you are in.

## Victoria

The Department of Health explains the public process on its [nursing and midwifery graduates](https://www.health.vic.gov.au/nursing-and-midwifery/nursing-and-midwifery-graduates) page. Public health services use the graduate nursing and midwifery program match, run with the Postgraduate Medical Council of Victoria. PMCV's site is [pmcv.com.au](https://www.pmcv.com.au/). The match is a preference system. It is not a list of interview questions, and this page will not invent one. Private employers may recruit outside the match. Read the current dates on those two sites before you lock a weekend to the form.

## Queensland

Queensland Health publishes the [registered nurse and midwife graduate program](https://www.careers.health.qld.gov.au/nursing-and-midwifery-careers/nurse-and-midwife-graduate-program) on its careers site. When that page was read in November 2026, it said the 2027 round had closed. The next round's dates will be on that page, not in a screenshot from last year.

## What to prepare without a leaked question bank

Have your registration timing straight. Have referees who have agreed. Have a preference list you could actually live with, including a second choice. Be able to talk about a shift without naming a patient, a colleague, or a hospital. That is the same boundary as the [submission rules](/submission-rules.html). The [first graduate year](/guides/first-graduate-year.html) page is about the months after an offer, still in general terms.

The [shop](/shop.html) has the creator's nursing student and new grad toolkit if you want templates. It is not an official application form for any state.
