## A minimum, not a ceiling

A nurse-to-patient ratio is the minimum number of nurses or midwives for the patients on that shift, in the wards the instrument covers. It is not a cap. Victoria's Department of Health describes the [Safe Patient Care (Nurse to Patient and Midwife to Patient Ratios) Act 2015](https://www.legislation.vic.gov.au/in-force/acts/safe-patient-care-nurse-patient-and-midwife-patient-ratios-act-2015) as minimum staffing for certain public services. A [2022 implementation guide](https://www.health.vic.gov.au/sites/default/files/2022-10/Safer%20patient%20care%20implementation%20guide.pdf) says the Act does not stop a hospital staffing above the number. [Section 7](https://classic.austlii.edu.au/au/legis/sa/consol_act/namtpra2025358/s7.html) of South Australia's Act, in the AustLII text, says a ratio is a minimum only, and that the nurse or midwife in charge may still be given patients if the total meets the ratio.

"Plus in charge" is not one national rule. New South Wales lists in-charge hours beside its [Safe Staffing Levels](https://www.health.nsw.gov.au/workforce/Pages/safe-staffing-levels.aspx). South Australia allows that nurse to take a load. An assistant in nursing is not automatically counted in the ratio. Read the instrument before you announce that the team leader is spare.

## Where it is written down

**Victoria.** The Department of Health's [legislation page](https://www.health.vic.gov.au/nursing-and-midwifery/nursing-and-midwifery-legislation-and-regulation) says the 2015 Act sets minimum staffing for certain publicly funded services. The in-force Act is on [legislation.vic.gov.au](https://www.legislation.vic.gov.au/in-force/acts/safe-patient-care-nurse-patient-and-midwife-patient-ratios-act-2015). The same page describes a hospital classification review, assented on 1 September 2026, with staged changes from 1 July 2027. The 2022 guide listed public mental health as outside the Act. On 5 November 2026 the [ANMF (Victorian Branch)](https://otr.anmfvic.asn.au/articles/an-historic-moment-for-mental-health-nurses/) said the exemption was still there, and that bed-based public mental health was staffed from its enterprise agreement. Open the Act for your ward.

**Queensland.** The [Hospital and Health Boards Regulation 2023](https://www.legislation.qld.gov.au/view/pdf/inforce/current/sl-2023-0100), current as at 5 December 2025, sets minimums for particular acute adult wards in listed public facilities: patients divided by 4 on mornings and afternoons, and by 7 at night. Under 1 becomes 1. Other fractions round to the nearest whole number, and one-half rounds down. Seven morning patients become a minimum of 2. Listed postnatal wards divide postnatal patients by 6 from the date in the schedule. That definition counts the person who has given birth and the newborn. [Queensland Health](https://www.performance.health.qld.gov.au/statewide-performance/planned-care/planned-surgery-capability) says those public acute ratios were first legislated on 1 July 2016. The schedule is not every ward.

**South Australia.** The [Nurse and Midwife to Patient Ratios Act 2025](https://www.legislation.sa.gov.au/lz?path=%2Fc%2Fa%2Fnurse+and+midwife+to+patient+ratios+act+2025) is in force. The ward ratios are in its schedules. Section 7, linked above, is the counting rule.

**Western Australia.** A [government statement on 16 November 2026](https://www.wa.gov.au/government/media-statements/Cook%20Labor%20Government/Nurse-to-patient-ratios-expanded-across-more-WA-public-hospitals-20261116) said the public rollout covers 91 metropolitan wards and all metropolitan emergency departments, with the final 33 wards starting that day. It names the hospitals in the phase, plans maternity and mental health for 2027, and calls the rollout a commitment of the Cook Labor Government. It does not point to an Act. Use the local policy and any agreement.

**New South Wales public hospitals.** [NSW Health](https://www.health.nsw.gov.au/workforce/Pages/safe-staffing-levels.aspx) is phasing in Safe Staffing Levels with the NSWNMA. Several inpatient areas are published at 1:4 in the morning, 1:4 in the afternoon and 1:7 at night, on occupied beds, with in-charge hours separate. Emergency rows include 1:1 for generally occupied resuscitation beds and 1:3 for generally occupied treatment spaces. This page did not find a Victorian-style statute on that page. Maternity was still being discussed.

**Australian Capital Territory.** The [ACT ratios page](https://www.act.gov.au/open/nurse-and-midwife-patient-ratios), updated 17 December 2025, says the public-sector agreements outline mandated minimums that Canberra Health Services implements. A [release on 18 February 2026](https://www.cmtedd.act.gov.au/open_government/inform/act_government_media_releases/rachel-stephen-smith-mla-media-releases/2026/final-rollout-of-new-nurse-and-midwife-to-patient-ratios-commences) described the last part of phase two. That is an agreement and a framework, not an Act.

**Northern Territory.** Checked on 18 November 2026, this page could not verify a legislated ratio from an NT statute or from [NT Health's nursing page](https://health.nt.gov.au/professionals/nursing-and-midwifery/about-nursing-and-midwifery). Open the local agreement rather than copying another state. [Pay and penalty rates](/guides/pay-and-penalty-rates.html) makes the same point.

## Public systems and private employers

Victoria, Queensland and South Australia, above, are public-sector laws. Western Australia's statement and the NSW Health page are public systems. A private hospital is usually an agreement or a policy, and that agreement may contain no ratio at all.

On 17 November 2026, [Coast Community News](https://coastcommunitynews.com.au/central-coast/news/2026/11/nurses-strike-in-face-of-healthe-care-offer/) reported an NSWNMA stop-work on 11 November at three Central Coast Healthe Care private hospitals, described as protected industrial action, with pay and ratios among the issues. It reported a vote from 9am on 19 November to 4pm on 23 November 2026 across 13 Healthe Care hospitals, and that members said the offer had no ratios. Healthe Care had not responded in the article. How a vote works is in [stop-works, strikes and EBA votes](/guides/enterprise-bargaining-and-industrial-action.html).

## The Victorian election, stated flatly

The [Parliament of Victoria](https://www.parliament.vic.gov.au/election) says the election is on Saturday 28 November 2026, and that early voting opens on Wednesday 18 November 2026. That is a calendar fact.

On 5 November 2026, [Victorian Labor](https://www.viclabor.org.au/news/labor-will-deliver-better-mental-health-care-with-nurse-to-patient-ratios) said a re-elected Carroll Labor government would legislate mental health ratios: acute adult, and acute child and youth, at 1:4 morning and evening and 1:8 at night; secure extended care the same; high dependency at 1:2. It described 102 million dollars, almost 160 more mental health nurses, and 30 new graduate places. The ANMF (Victorian Branch) described the acute, child and adolescent, and secure extended care figures as including a supernumerary in-charge nurse. Labor's post does not say "in charge". A promise is not the Act.

The Liberal and Nationals' [letter to Tandem](https://tandemcarers.org.au/common/Uploaded%20files/2026Election/261023_LNP-response-to-scorecard-questionsv2.pdf), dated 23 October 2026, promises a Mental Health Workforce Strategy within 100 days and a 50 million dollar Targeted Mental Health Workforce Fund, plus further commitments before election day. It does not mention ratios. It is a reply to a carers' group, not a whole health policy. This page prefers neither statement.

## When the board and the people do not match

Tell the person in charge: the shift is below the staffing the instrument requires, and you are escalating it. Use the path your service has named. Note the time and the staffing in private, with no patient names, bed numbers, diagnoses or photos of the board. Use the incident process and, where it is a safety matter, the work health and safety process. A union can explain the industrial step if you are a member. Students tell the facilitator and the university. A placement is not the ratio. See [your first clinical placement](/guides/first-clinical-placement.html).

This website is not the report. [When and how to speak up](/guides/speaking-up-about-safety.html) is the overview. If you are not safe to continue, or not safe to drive, say so before you are in the car. [Fatigue and night shift](/guides/fatigue-and-night-shift-sleep.html) is the companion, not personal advice. Short staffing is a design problem. Nurse & Midwife Support is [1800 667 877](https://www.nmsupport.org.au/). Lifeline is [13 11 14](https://www.lifeline.org.au/). If there is immediate danger, call 000. The full moon will not staff the ward. The document will, when it applies.
