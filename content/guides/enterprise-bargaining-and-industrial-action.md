## The award, then the agreement

A modern award sets minimum pay and conditions. An enterprise agreement is the deal for one employer, or a group, and it can apply instead of the award. Public hospitals often use a state agreement. Private hospitals often use their own. [Pay and penalty rates](/guides/pay-and-penalty-rates.html) is the map. The Ombudsman explains national-system [agreements](https://www.fairwork.gov.au/employment-conditions/agreements). "We're on the EBA" is not a title. Ask for the name and the nominal expiry date.

Bargaining is the negotiation for the next agreement. In the national system, a union or another bargaining representative can act for employees. The Ombudsman's page on [unions in enterprise bargaining](https://www.fairwork.gov.au/employment-conditions/representational-rights-and-responsibilities-in-the-workplace/the-role-of-unions-in-enterprise-bargaining) is the outline. The Fair Work Commission deals with bargaining applications, ballots and later approval. A log of claims is a list. An offer stays a proposal until the vote, and until the commission that registers agreements has finished its own step.

## Protected industrial action, in general terms

The Fair Work Act distinguishes protected industrial action from unprotected action. Protected action meets the Act's conditions during bargaining. Use the Ombudsman's [industrial action page](https://www.fairwork.gov.au/employment-conditions/representational-rights-and-responsibilities-in-the-workplace/industrial-action) and [fact sheet](https://www.fairwork.gov.au/tools-and-resources/fact-sheets/rights-and-obligations/industrial-action) together. This summary is not the Act.

Employee claim action generally needs a protected action ballot, claims about permitted matters, notice, and parties genuinely trying to reach agreement. The Commission orders conciliation during the ballot period. The fact sheet says action is not protected if it is taken before the nominal expiry date in the way the Act forbids. It describes at least three working days' written notice for a single-enterprise agreement, and at least 120 hours for a multi-enterprise agreement, unless the order sets longer.

[When action is authorised](https://www.fwc.gov.au/when-industrial-action-authorised) says the ballot counts only if at least half the roll votes, more than half the valid votes approve, and the action starts within 30 days, or a longer period the Commission allows. The [procedure page](https://www.fwc.gov.au/workplace-disputes/other-workplace-disputes/industrial-action/protected-and-unprotected-industrial) is the rest. Annoyance in the tea room is not that ballot.

The Commission can suspend or end protected action if it is likely to endanger life, personal safety, health or welfare, or in the other cases the Ombudsman lists. Patient safety is why that power exists. Unprotected action does not carry the same protections. Penalties belong to the Act and a court.

## A state system is a different Act

Tasmanian State Service agreements are registered by the [Tasmanian Industrial Commission](https://www.tic.tas.gov.au/public_sector_agreements). The [Department of Premier and Cabinet](https://www.dpac.tas.gov.au/divisions/ssmo/awards_and_agreements) says they are negotiated with the unions that are party to them. If that is your employer, do not assume the Fair Work notice periods above. Ask which Act applies. Private national-system employers are the usual home of those Fair Work rules. A report that says "protected industrial action" is using that language. The notice still says what was authorised.

The [Tasmanian negotiations page](https://www.tas.gov.au/tss-negotiations) says the Head of the State Service offered the ANMF and HACSU, on 30 October 2026, a three-year deal: 3 per cent, then 2.75 per cent, then 2.5 per cent. The table's last scheduled increase is 1 December 2025, and the next anticipated date is 1 December 2026. Three dates are not one first-year rise. The [ANMF, on 17 November 2026](https://anmj.org.au/anmf-stands-with-tasmanian-nurses-and-midwives-over-inadequate-eba-offer/), called that 8.25 per cent over three years and reported a 15-minute walk-off at the Royal Hobart Hospital. The [ABC, on 7 November 2026](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-07/tasmania-nurses-rally-over-offer-pay-conditions/107359318), reported an earlier stop-work there and at the Repatriation Centre, and a spokesperson saying the government was working with the unions. The ABC said the agreement expired in June. ANMF has said personal impact days in the offer were limited to emergency, intensive care and mental health. This page does not rank it.

[Coast Community News](https://coastcommunitynews.com.au/central-coast/news/2026/11/nurses-strike-in-face-of-healthe-care-offer/) reported on 17 November 2026 that NSWNMA members at three Central Coast Healthe Care hospitals stopped work for two and a half hours on 11 November, described as protected action over pay and ratios. It reported a vote from 9am on 19 November to 4pm on 23 November 2026 across 13 Healthe Care hospitals, and that members said the offer had no ratios. Healthe Care had not commented. ## Reading the offer before you mark it

In the national system the employer asks covered employees to vote. The Commission's [voting process](https://www.fwc.gov.au/voting-process) describes seven clear days of access, a copy of the text, notice of the vote, and an explanation of the effect of the terms. [Genuine agreement](https://www.fwc.gov.au/genuine-agreement) says a single-enterprise agreement is made when a majority of those who cast a valid vote approve it. Commission approval comes after that. A majority on the day is not, by itself, an agreement in force.

Before you mark a box, find your classification and the dates on its pay table. Find any staffing or ratio clause, including a silence where you expected one. Find whether back pay is written down, and from which date. Find the nominal expiry date. In the national system that date matters next time. If a clause says another document applies, open it. A Tasmanian State Service ballot follows that service's notice.

You may join a union, or not. The Ombudsman calls that freedom of association, on the [role of unions](https://www.fairwork.gov.au/employment-conditions/representational-rights-and-responsibilities-in-the-workplace/role-of-unions-and-employer-associations) page. In the national system, adverse action because you are a member, or because you are not, or because you did or did not take part in lawful industrial activity, is unlawful. State public sector staff should also check their commission. A union can explain an offer. It cannot mark your ballot.

## Students, and the codes

A vocational placement that meets the Fair Work test is not employment and does not carry award wages. The [student placements fact sheet](https://www.fairwork.gov.au/tools-and-resources/fact-sheets/unpaid-work/student-placements) requires a placement, no entitlement to pay, a course requirement, and an authorised provider. If one is missing, it may be employment. This page will not tell a student to cross a stop-work or to stay away. The university and the facilitator set the placement rules. [Your first clinical placement](/guides/first-clinical-placement.html) is that ordinary process. Paid work as an assistant in nursing is a different job. The fact sheet says award or agreement work is not a student placement.

The NMBA [codes and standards](https://www.nursingmidwiferyboard.gov.au/Codes-Guidelines-Statements/Professional-standards.aspx) apply while you are practising. A walk-off is time you are not practising. A ban while you stay on the shift is still practice. Do not post a patient, a colleague or a ward. [Social media and professionalism](/guides/social-media-and-professionalism.html) is that line. [Speaking up about safety](/guides/speaking-up-about-safety.html) is the reporting overview.

If the dispute follows you home, you do not have to settle it online. Nurse & Midwife Support is [1800 667 877](https://www.nmsupport.org.au/). Lifeline is [13 11 14](https://www.lifeline.org.au/). A yes and a no are both allowed to be yours. This page will not pick one.
