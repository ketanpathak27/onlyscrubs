The Nursing and Midwifery Board of Australia asks you to do continuing professional development and to be able to show it if you are audited. The learning and the record are different jobs. This page is about the record. It is general information only, checked against the Board's pages on 17 November 2026. It is not clinical advice, and it is not an Ahpra submission.

The hours themselves are explained in [CPD requirements for new grads](/guides/cpd-for-new-grads.html). The short version, from the NMBA's [understanding CPD](https://www.nursingmidwiferyboard.gov.au/Registration-Standards/Understanding-CPD-for-nurses-and-midwives) page and the [7 April 2026 renewal notice](https://www.nursingmidwiferyboard.gov.au/News/2026-04-07-Registration-renewal-opens): enrolled nurses, registered nurses and midwives complete a minimum of 20 hours in a registration period. Endorsements can add more. Read the standard before you declare a number.

## What a useful record contains

Write it down when you do it, not in May when the portal is open and your memory is a rumour. A line that would survive an audit usually has:

- the date
- what you did, in a few plain words
- how many hours you are counting
- why it relates to the work you actually do

"Handover workshop, 2 hours, because my handovers were too long" is a record. "Did some study" is not. Do not put a patient's name, a bed number, or a workplace story in the note. The record is about your learning, not a shift report.

The studies that qualified you for registration do not count as CPD the first time you renew. That sentence is in the NMBA's renewal notice. A graduate program study day after you are registered can count when it meets the standard. Log it on the day.

## Where to keep it

A notebook, a folder, or the tally on this page. The tally uses this browser's local storage. It is not uploaded to OnlyScrubs, and it is not sent to the NMBA. Clear the browser, or use another phone, and the list is gone. If the hours matter, keep a copy somewhere you control as well.

The [nursing toolkit on the shop](/shop.html) includes a grad year and CPD tracker among its templates. That is the creator's own file, not an NMBA product. Use it if you want a printable sheet. The standard is still the Board's page.

## When you renew

You declare that you have met the standard. You do not attach the folder unless you are audited. Keep the evidence for the period the standard describes. If you are not sure you have the hours, do not tick the declaration and hope. The portal and the standard are the place to sort that out, not a group chat.

Twenty hours is a floor for most registrants, not a prize. Relevant and recorded beats a heroic number you cannot explain.
