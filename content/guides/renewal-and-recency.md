Renewal is the annual declaration that you still meet the registration standards. Recency of practice is one of those standards: have you actually practised recently enough? This page is general information only, checked on 17 November 2026. It is not a renewal, not a fee invoice, and not clinical advice. If the NMBA page and this page disagree, use theirs.

## When it is due

Nurses and midwives renew through the Ahpra portal. The NMBA's [7 April 2026 renewal notice](https://www.nursingmidwiferyboard.gov.au/News/2026-04-07-Registration-renewal-opens) told registrants to renew by 31 May 2026 to avoid delays and late fees. The registration year runs to 31 May. The countdown on this page counts days until the next 31 May on your device. It does not know whether you have already renewed.

If you hold nursing and midwifery, renew everything you need. The same notice says one renewal fee covers both nursing and midwifery registration. Endorsements are extra to check, not extra to ignore.

## What the fee schedule says

The [NMBA fees page](https://www.nursingmidwiferyboard.gov.au/Registration-and-Endorsement/Fees.aspx) is the schedule. The version effective 9 October 2026 lists:

- general registration at A$201 nationally
- A$193 if your principal place of practice is New South Wales, after an A$8 rebate
- a late renewal fee of A$50 for general registration
- an application fee of A$101 for recent graduates of approved programs, which is not the same thing as the annual renewal

Those figures are copied here so the page is useful. They will change. Pay the amount the portal shows on the day. The April 2026 news announcement described an earlier A$193 national fee for the 2026/27 period. The October schedule is the later document, so that is the one this page uses.

## Recency of practice

The NMBA [recency of practice fact sheet](https://www.nursingmidwiferyboard.gov.au/Codes-Guidelines-Statements/FAQ/recency-of-practice.aspx) says you need evidence of practice equivalent to a minimum of 450 hours within the past five years. The hours do not have to be continuous. You can add them up.

You declare that you meet it when you renew. You do not upload the evidence every year. If you are audited, you need it. The fact sheet says to keep evidence for at least five years. A service statement, pay slips, an income statement, or other documents that show hours and dates are the examples the Board gives. What they accept is their decision.

If you are registered as both a nurse and a midwife, the fact sheet says you need the equivalent of 450 hours in each, unless you can show that a piece of work was practice in both. Do not assume a mixed roster covers both columns.

If you are employed and still short of 450 hours, you declare that you do not meet recency. The renewal is then assessed. Do not guess the box. The fact sheet points to re-entry to practice for the next step.

## CPD sits next to this

Renewal also asks whether you met the CPD standard. Recording those hours is a separate habit, covered in [recording your NMBA CPD](/guides/recording-your-cpd.html). The first application for registration, if you are still a student, is [AHPRA registration as a new grad](/guides/ahpra-registration-new-grad.html).

Nothing on this page lodges a renewal. The portal does that. The countdown is a calendar, not a reminder from Ahpra.
