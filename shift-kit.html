Shift Kit

Small tools for the shift before, during and after the chaos.

Before shift

  • Check uniform, badge, shoes, watch, pen, water bottle and snacks before leaving.
  • Bring a spare pen. Then bring another spare pen, because optimism has limits.
  • Review your local policies and scope. OnlyScrubs is not a substitute for workplace guidance.
  • Put your phone on a mode that lets urgent personal contacts through without turning your pocket into a nightclub.

During shift

  • Write down tasks as soon as you safely can. Memory is not a registered documentation system.
  • Escalate early when something is outside your role, competence or comfort.
  • Hydrate and eat when you can. Hero mode is not a sustainable roster pattern.
  • Keep patient, colleague and workplace details off public platforms.

General handover prompt

This is a general organisation prompt, not clinical advice. Use your workplace-approved handover format.

  • Who or what needs follow-up?
  • What has changed since the last handover?
  • What is time-sensitive?
  • What has already been escalated?
  • What still needs documentation or confirmation?

After shift reset

  • Change out of work gear when practical.
  • Drink water before your second emotional-support beverage.
  • Write down anything you need to follow up through proper workplace channels.
  • If the shift was rough, contact a trusted person or support service rather than carrying it alone.
