Plain-English notice

Privacy

OnlyScrubs is designed to collect as little information as possible. Please do not send patient information, clinical details, confidential workplace information, or information about another person without permission.

What may be collected

  • Your name or preferred name if you choose to provide it.
  • Your email address if you contact OnlyScrubs.
  • General scrub swap details such as size, colour, condition and general pickup area.
  • General feedback, resource ideas or anonymous humour submissions.

What not to send

  • Patient names, dates of birth, medical record numbers, images or clinical histories.
  • Stories that identify a patient, colleague, hospital, ward, clinic, employer or incident.
  • Health information about yourself or someone else unless it is truly necessary for your request.

How information is used

Information is used to reply to messages, manage Scrub Rack interest, improve resources, moderate submissions and send updates if you ask to receive them.

Before bigger features launch

If OnlyScrubs later adds web forms, donations, payments, user accounts or a public listing board, this privacy notice should be reviewed and expanded before launch.
