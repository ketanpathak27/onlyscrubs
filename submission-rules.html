Keep it funny, keep it safe

Submission rules

OnlyScrubs is here for harmless healthcare-worker humour and useful resources. Submissions may be edited, rejected or removed if they create privacy, professionalism, legal or workplace risk.

Allowed

  • Harmless jokes about shift life, pens, socks, coffee, badge reels, placement nerves and laundry piles.
  • General tips for students, new grads and healthcare workers.
  • Scrub swap details that do not expose private information.
  • General wellbeing resources and support signposting.

Not allowed

  • Patient stories, even if you think they are de-identified.
  • Patient images, clinical details, diagnoses, treatment details or incident details.
  • Hospital, ward, clinic, employer, colleague or patient identifiers.
  • Bullying, harassment, discrimination, sexual content, hate speech or workplace call-outs.
  • Clinical advice, diagnosis, treatment claims or testimonials about regulated health services.
  • Anything confidential, defamatory, misleading or likely to breach professional obligations.

The simple test

If a patient, colleague, manager, regulator or future employer could recognise the person, place or event, do not submit it.
