Placement without panic packing

Student Bay

A starter guide for showing up prepared, curious and safely within your scope.

Placement packing checklist

  • Approved uniform or scrubs, plus a backup if you have one.
  • Comfortable closed shoes that meet placement requirements.
  • ID badge, placement documents and any required access details.
  • Two pens, small notebook, water bottle and food you can eat quickly.
  • Plain jumper or layers if allowed by site policy.
  • Any required vaccinations, checks or compliance documents as directed by your school or placement site.

First-day etiquette

  • Arrive early enough to get lost once and still be on time.
  • Introduce yourself clearly: name, course, year level and who you are allocated to.
  • Ask before doing, especially when you are unsure about scope or local process.
  • Do not post about placement, patients, staff, locations or incidents online.
  • Reflect privately and professionally. Public venting can become a career-shaped problem.

Questions worth asking

  • "What is the best way for me to communicate tasks or concerns here?"
  • "What should I read before tomorrow?"
  • "Is there anything site-specific I should know about documentation, escalation or breaks?"
  • "Can I get feedback on one thing I did well and one thing to improve?"
