Scrub Rack

A low-drama place for clean scrub gear to find its next shift.

How it works

  1. Send what you have or need.
    Include size, colour, condition, suburb or pickup area, and whether it is donation, request or swap.
  2. Keep it clean and simple.
    No employer details, no patient stories, no explanations about personal hardship unless you choose to share them privately.
  3. OnlyScrubs connects interest.
    The first version runs by email so the project can stay small, safe and manageable while demand is tested.

What belongs here

  • Clean scrub tops and pants in usable condition.
  • Unused scrub caps, compression socks or simple placement gear.
  • Student placement starter bundles.
  • Local swap requests for colour or size changes.

What does not belong here

  • Soiled, damaged or unsafe items.
  • Items with hospital, employer or person-identifying embroidery unless removed.
  • Medical devices, medications, clinical supplies or anything requiring workplace approval.
  • Patient information, workplace incidents or confidential details.

Use the Scrub Rack

Email hello@onlyscrubs.org with the subject line Scrub Rack.
