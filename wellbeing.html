Support signposts

Wellbeing

Not therapy, not medical advice. Just a useful place to find support links when the shift follows you home.

If there is immediate danger

In Australia, call 000 for urgent police, fire or ambulance help.

Australian support services

Lifeline - 13 11 14 24/7 crisis support in Australia. Beyond Blue - 1300 22 4636 24/7 mental health support service. Nurse & Midwife Support - 1800 667 877 24/7 support for nurses, midwives and students in Australia.

Workplace support

  • Use your employer's Employee Assistance Program if available.
  • Talk to your educator, preceptor, supervisor or union/professional association when appropriate.
  • Escalate safety concerns through local workplace processes.
